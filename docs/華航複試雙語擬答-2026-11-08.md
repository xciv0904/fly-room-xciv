## 3. 飛機無法起飛，旅客詢問原因，妳會說實話嗎？

Would you tell passengers the truth about why their flight cannot depart?

分類：實戰回顧


中文擬答：

會，我會誠實說明已確認、可對外傳達的資訊。如果原因還在確認，我會直接說目前仍在評估，不用猜測填補空白，也不因為可能涉及人為因素就刻意含糊。我會向負責人員確認最新狀況，讓旅客知道有新資訊會再更新，同時了解是否有轉機、用藥或其他急迫需求。對我而言，誠實也包括清楚說明目前不知道什麼。


English draft:

I would check for updates and ask about urgent needs, such as connections or medication. Being honest also means clearly explaining what we do not yet know.


回答提醒：修正舊答『人為疏失說籠統一點』。確認是為了準確，不是隱瞞已確認的重要資訊。

I would return with new information rather than leave them feeling ignored.

我會先保持穩定，了解他不滿的原因，把注意力放在可處理的需求。我不會搶手機、爭辯或用自己的手機反拍，也會留意其他旅客的隱私與現場安全。若持續干擾工作或威脅人員，我會依公司程序請座艙長支援，清楚表達界線，並如實記錄經過。被拍攝的壓力不能讓我做出影響安全或不專業的反應。


English draft:

I would stay composed, understand the complaint, and focus on what can be resolved. I would not grab the phone, argue, or film back. I would also consider other passengers' privacy and safety. If the behaviour disrupts duties or threatens anyone, I would seek the purser's support under company procedures and record the facts accurately. The pressure of being filmed should not drive an unsafe or unprofessional response.


回答提醒：不要自行斷言所有拍攝都違法；依現場行為、公司規範與程序處理。

I would follow training and procedures and ask an appropriate crew member or supervisor when unsure. Taking initiative does not mean doing everything alone; it means actively finding the safe and correct way forward.

## 39. 飯店櫃檯與空服員最大的不同是什麼？妳如何適應？

English draft:

Both roles require listening, communication, and teamwork. Cabin crew also have direct safety, security, and emergency responsibilities in a confined environment with different resources. My hotel experience helps me stay composed with guests, but it does not replace aviation training. I would adapt as a beginner: learn the official procedures, practise, and accept feedback, while bringing the service attitude I have already developed.


回答提醒：不要只回答一個在地上、一個在空中；講出責任、資源與學習方式。
