## 六個故事

### S-A 加拿大旅客：連假租車

想在台北車站附近租車 → 先打電話確認 → 已租完 → 改找飯店附近公司、協助預約 → 隔天回來道謝。

I called ahead during a holiday, found the original rental office fully booked, and helped arrange a reservation near the hotel.

可回答：動機、主動服務、解決問題、成就感

事實界線：只提供地址還沒排除障礙；重點是多確認了車況。

### S-B 哥倫比亞旅客：中文線上報到

中文介面與手機操作卡住 → 陪她核對資料、翻譯畫面 → 一步一步完成線上報到。

I checked her details and translated the Chinese instructions step by step until she completed online check-in.

可回答：跨文化、語言、細心、數位工具、人員價值

事實界線：與加拿大租車事件分開；主動協助仍要尊重同意與隱私。

### S-C 孕婦旅客：靠枕與近電梯房間

注意到旅客站得不舒服 → 準備靠枕、協助安排近電梯房間 → 旅客在評論提到這份照顧。

I noticed her discomfort and helped with a cushion and a room near the lift. She later mentioned the care in a review.

可回答：觀察力、細心、需求預判、服務細節

事實界線：觀察不等於診斷；轉為機上情境時，先詢問需求再協助。

### S-D 韓國醉酒旅客：私人電話界線

旅客索取私人電話 → 用韓文有禮拒絕 → 提供飯店公務電話 → 提醒同行導遊留意。

I declined politely in Korean, offered the hotel’s official number, and alerted the tour guide.

可回答：韓文、專業界線、困難旅客、穩定溝通

事實界線：沒有把假設的碰觸、暴力或受傷加進真實事件。

### S-E 高卡會員：禮遇說明不完整

入住時禮遇未完整說明 → 旅客不滿 → 不歸咎旅客趕時間 → 致歉、逐項補說明與確認需求。

I took responsibility for the incomplete explanation, apologised, and went through the benefits with the guest.

可回答：失誤、客訴、責任、負面回饋、改善

事實界線：不虛構補償金額、官方表揚或從此零失誤。

### S-F 哭泣旅客：房型不足找替代

旅客已錯過班機，房型問題成為最後壓力 → 先聽完 → 確認需要休息 → 以沙發床作為可行替代。

I listened to what had happened and helped arrange a sofa bed when her preferred room was unavailable.

可回答：同理、無法滿足要求、客訴、資源限制

事實界線：飯店可行方案不直接搬到機上；先核對安全、資源與權限。

If [the request] is unavailable, I would offer [a workable alternative] within my authority and seek appropriate support where needed. I would explain the next step and follow up, rather than leave the passenger without a response.

怎麼填：餐點缺漏：核對預訂與可用餐點；Wi-Fi：核對方案與資格。替代方案依題目換。

I would check their final destination and onward connection.

不是departure出發，也不是必然等於眼前這一段的抵達地。

### 35 itinerary：完整行程

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

check the itinerary

I would check the itinerary before explaining transfer options.

可包含多個航段與日期；不要只看一個航班。

### 36 connection：轉機銜接、連線

出現於14/189則；合計14次。

a connecting flight / a tight connection

I would check whether the delay affects their connecting flight.

航空題指轉機；Wi-Fi題可能指網路連線。

### 37 transit：過境、中轉

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

transit requirements

I would check the itinerary and refer transit questions to the proper channel.

入境與過境條件可能不同；不憑記憶替旅客保證。

### 38 departure：出發、起飛

出現於6/189則；合計6次。

a confirmed departure time

I do not have a confirmed departure time yet.

注意讀音與拼字；和destination目的地不同。

### 39 evacuation：緊急撤離

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

an emergency evacuation

I would follow my training during an emergency evacuation.

面試談受訓與分工，不編造未受訓的操作口令。

### 40 precaution：預防措施

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

a safety precaution

I would explain that the restriction is a safety precaution.

說明措施目的，不代表可以自行更動規則。

### 41 allergen：過敏原

出現於1/189則；合計3次。

check allergen information

I would check the meal information without promising it is allergen-free.

看不見或移除食材都不能保證沒有接觸過敏原。[S11]

### 42 dietary restriction：飲食限制

出現於1/189則；合計1次。

ask about dietary restrictions

I would ask about dietary restrictions before suggesting a meal.

比vegetarian更廣，可能與宗教、健康或個人需求有關。

### 43 contamination：污染；餐食情境可指沾染

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

cross-contamination

I would not assume that removing an ingredient prevents cross-contamination.

食材移除後仍可能接觸過敏原，需查核正式資訊。[S11]

### 44 purser：座艙長

出現於4/189則；合計4次。

notify the purser

I would notify the purser about the continuing safety concern.

遇到需要通報的情況，說明觀察到什麼與需要什麼支援。

### 45 overhead compartment：上方行李置物櫃

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

store a bag in the overhead compartment

I would check that baggage is stored according to procedure.

行動電源的收放須另依現行規定，不因行李放這裡就跟著放。

### 46 aisle：走道

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

keep the aisle clear

I would ask passengers to keep the aisle clear.

注意拼字；不是isle島嶼。

### 47 sustainability：永續、可持續性

出現於1/189則；合計1次。

support sustainability

I would support sustainability without overstating the benefits.

和sustainable合為同一組；不等於零碳排。

### 48 handover：交接

出現於14/189則；合計14次。

a clear handover

I would give a clear handover so the request was not lost.

交接要有狀況、已做事項、待辦與接手人。

### 49 accountability：對結果負責、可追究責任

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

be accountable for…

I would be accountable for my mistake and take steps to correct it.

responsibility偏職責；accountability強調對行動與結果負責。

### 50 contingency：備案、突發狀況的應變安排

出現於0/189則；合計0次。

a contingency plan

I would check the contingency plan instead of improvising.

不是自己發明程序；先理解核准的備案。

## 常見句型

### I would first…：先做什麼（20/189則）

I would first confirm what the passenger needs.

我會先確認旅客需要什麼。

適用假設情境；下一句要給具體行動，不再堆first、second。

### I would check…：查核條件（16/189則）

I would check whether the option is available.

我會確認這個選項目前能否提供。

whether接是否；check with接核對對象，如the crew。

### I would ask…：詢問／要求配合（14/189則）

I would ask the passenger to sit down and fasten their seat belt.

我會請旅客就座並繫好安全帶。

ask someone to +原形動詞；不要少了to。

### I would explain…：清楚說明（23/189則）

I would explain what is confirmed and what is still being checked.

我會說明已確認的內容，以及仍在查核的部分。

避免只說I would explain politely卻沒有說明內容。

### I would not…：明確界線（36/189則）

I would not promise a service before confirming it is available.

我不會在確認可提供之前，就先保證有這項服務。

說完不做什麼，再接能做的事；不用每題都以否定開頭。

### rather than…：表達判斷差異（50/189則）

I would seek support rather than guess about safety.

涉及安全時，我會尋求支援，而不是猜測。

rather than後接與前面平行的動作；這裡seek與guess平行。

## 短追問

### 旅客說別家可以用行動電源

我會說明本公司的安全規範，提供可行選項，不替他航下結論。

I would explain our safety requirements and check available options.

### 餐點成分不確定、有過敏疑慮

先核對正式資料與組員；不憑外觀猜測、不保證完全無過敏原。[S11]

I would verify the meal information without guaranteeing an allergen-free meal.

### 旅客拿手機拍攝、威脅公審

穩定處理需求、留意安全與其他人隱私；不搶手機或反拍。

I would address the complaint calmly and seek support if duties or safety were affected.

### 輪班或長程飛行很疲倦

管理休息；若影響安全執勤，如實反映並依程序尋求評估。

If fatigue affected my ability to work safely, I would report it honestly.

### 旅客指定查明星是否搭同班機

沒有工作目的就不查、不確認、不轉傳。

I would not access passenger records for a personal request.

### 能不能用私人AI翻譯旅客資料

不把個資送進未核准工具；重要內容須核對並尋求適當協助。

I would use approved tools and verify important information.

### 旅客問多久一定起飛

只說已確認時間；沒有時間就明說仍在確認，不自行猜。

I do not have a confirmed departure time yet; I would seek an update.

### 為什麼不是其他航空公司

尊重各家公司，回到自己研究華航的具體理由與能投入的工作。

I respect each airline; my choice is based on what I have learned about China Airlines.

### 妳有什麼需要改善

可談正在練習精簡表達：先回答重點，再用一件事證明，不背假缺點。

I am practising more concise answers: one clear point, one relevant example, and a short takeaway.

### 公司建議想不到新點子

可延伸服務資訊：把限制、適用資格與人工協助入口說得更清楚，不宣稱從未有人做。

I would make eligibility, limits, and the next help channel easier to understand.
