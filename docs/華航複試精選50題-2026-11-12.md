中文擬答：

我認為最大的不同是，空服員除了提供服務，更肩負旅客的安全責任。
在飯店工作期間，我已經習慣接待不同國籍的旅客，也學會在忙碌和突發狀況下保持冷靜、與同事合作。
不過，我知道機艙環境有更嚴格的安全規範和團隊分工。所以我會以謙虛的態度接受訓練，熟悉標準作業程序，並積極與組員配合，讓自己從服務人員成長為合格的客艙安全專業人員。


English draft:

I believe the biggest difference is that cabin crew are responsible not only for service, but also for passenger safety.
Working at Hyatt has taught me to communicate with international guests, stay calm under pressure, and solve problems with my team.
However, I understand that aviation has stricter safety procedures and requires close teamwork. I would take training seriously, follow procedures carefully, and work closely with the crew to adapt to this new role.


#### 備選・客艙環境與安全職責

中文擬答：

Both roles require listening, communication, and teamwork. Cabin crew also have direct safety, security, and emergency responsibilities in a confined environment with different resources. My hotel experience helps me stay composed with guests, but it does not replace aviation training. I would adapt as a beginner: learn the official procedures, practise, and accept feedback, while bringing the service attitude I have already developed.

## 誠實溝通與責任


### 9. 飛機無法起飛，旅客詢問原因，妳會說實話嗎？

Would you tell passengers the truth about why their flight cannot depart?

考察分類：誠實溝通與責任｜題型來源：實戰回顧


中文擬答：

會，我會誠實說明已確認、可對外傳達的資訊。如果原因還在確認，我會直接說目前仍在評估，不用猜測填補空白，也不因為可能涉及人為因素就刻意含糊。我會向負責人員確認最新狀況，讓旅客知道有新資訊會再更新，同時了解是否有轉機、用藥或其他急迫需求。對我而言，誠實也包括清楚說明目前不知道什麼。


English draft:

Yes. I would share confirmed information honestly and follow the crew's communication guidance. If the cause is still being assessed, I would say so rather than guess or hide it. I would check for updates and ask about urgent needs, such as connections or medication. Being honest also means clearly explaining what we do not yet know.


回答提醒：修正舊答『人為疏失說籠統一點』。確認是為了準確，不是隱瞞已確認的重要資訊。

### 10. 已確認是公司作業疏失造成延誤，妳會告訴旅客嗎？

中文擬答：

會，我不會把已確認的作業問題改說成天候或其他原因。我會先確認正確的說明，向旅客交代目前的原因、處理進度與下一次更新，並為造成的不便致歉。若責任細節還在釐清，我會清楚區分，不自行指名責怪某位同事。我認為維護公司的形象，需要靠負責任地處理問題，而不是提供不實資訊。


English draft:

Yes. I would not describe a confirmed operational error as bad weather or invent another cause. I would verify the accurate explanation, acknowledge the inconvenience, and share the current action and next update. If individual responsibility was still being reviewed, I would say so rather than blame a colleague. I believe protecting the company's reputation requires an accountable response and truthful information.
